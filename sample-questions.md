# Sample Questions for AskTheArchive

Use these to test and demo the system. Organised by document and type.

## PDPA and Consent
- What are the exceptions to consent under the PDPA?
- When can an organisation collect personal data without consent?
- What is the Legitimate Interests Exception and when does it apply?
- What safeguards should be in place when relying on legitimate interests?
- Follow-up: How does that apply to public agencies?

## Healthcare Sector
- What are the PDPA obligations for healthcare organisations?
- How should healthcare organisations handle patient data?
- What are the consent requirements for healthcare data collection?
- Follow-up: What are the exceptions to that?

## Data Breach
- What are the breach notification requirements under PDPA?
- When must an organisation notify the PDPC of a data breach?
- What counts as a notifiable data breach?
- Follow-up: How long do we have to report it?

## Cybersecurity
- What are the key cybersecurity obligations for organisations in Singapore?
- What does the Cybersecurity Act require of critical information infrastructure owners?

## Questions that should return "I couldn't find this"
These test the anti-hallucination fallback:
- What is the weather forecast for Singapore tomorrow?
- Who won the 2024 Singapore general election?
- What is the best restaurant in Orchard Road?
- How do I apply for a HDB flat?
